About

I am a researcher in philosophy of science at the University of Turin. My work sits at the intersection of analytic philosophy and empirical research methods.

My main interest is understanding how the philosophy of science community works as an epistemic community: how knowledge circulates, how diversity shapes intellectual outcomes, and how the field has evolved over time. I approach these questions with the tools of bibliometrics and computational social science.

Alongside my research I work on data analysis projects to continue experimenting an learing new things.

02

Research

INT. 01

Scientometrics & Bibliometrics

Quantitative study of the philosophy of science literature: citation patterns, intellectual structure, and the dynamics of academic influence over time.

INT. 02

Epistemic Networks & Diversity

How does team diversity affect the epistemic output of research groups? Investigating the diversity thesis in philosophy of science through empirical and bibliometric evidence.

INT. 03

Agent-Based Modeling

Formal models of epistemic communities: extending classic results an building new models to explore how social structure shapes collective inquiry.
